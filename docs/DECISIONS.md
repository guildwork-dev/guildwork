# Decisions

Owner decisions of the project and the ruling log ([12](architecture/12-open-source-and-project.md) section 8). Architecture decisions are separate files under `docs/adr/`, indexed in [09](architecture/09-decisions.md). Statements about contracts and licenses are not legal advice, but the reading of the quoted texts with references ([12](architecture/12-open-source-and-project.md) section 7). Owner statements are summarized here, not quoted.

## 3. Owner decisions of the project

Only money, accounts, data going outward, law, and real product questions. One recommendation with rationale per item; only the project owner can decide.

### 3.1 Open

Currently no owner item is open (as of 2026-11-01, after O28). New owner items are added here as a table with the columns number, question, recommendation, and rationale.
